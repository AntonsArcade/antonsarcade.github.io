CROCALARIO
Rescue Princess Lilia

Crocalario the crocodile crosses 4 worlds (32 levels!) to save Princess Lilia for good — from the sunny swamp, into the deep blue sea, through a volcano, and up to a castle bridge. Grubba the Hippo, Sylvia the Seal, King Bat, and finally Baron Snapjaw each snatch her away in turn, and every world hits harder than the last. Stomp tiny elephants and snapping turtles, or throw a bird to take enemies out from a distance once you're powered up. Lucky blocks hide a turtle mushroom (grow big) or, once you're big, a bird feather — press X to throw. Some pipes lead to secret rooms — stand on top and press Down (or ▼) to dive in. Rumor has it one very well-hidden pipe skips whole worlds...
Arrows/WASD to move · Space/Up to jump · X to throw a bird · Down on a pipe for secrets
