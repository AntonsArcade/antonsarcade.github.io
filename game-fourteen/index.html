OUTLAW CITY

CAR CHAOS

You live in your car. Cruise the city lobbing bombs at people, cops and buildings. Crimes build your pending bounty; drive back to the green BASE to bank it. Reach ★★★★★ and bank it alive. Drive to the purple BLACK MARKET to buy bombs, mega bombs and faster cars. Heists are real: press R at the JEWELRY STORE, BANK or AIRPORT, bomb the vault open, grab every loot bag before time runs out, then escape to the BASE for x2. The richer and higher-ranked you get, the harder it gets. Fly the jet from the airport!

WASD / Arrows drive · Shift nitro · Mouse aim · Click / Space throw bomb
Q switch bomb · E enter shop · R start heist · E enter/exit jet · P pause · Desktop keyboard + mouse
