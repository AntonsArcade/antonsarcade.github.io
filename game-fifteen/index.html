How to play

Knock all 7 bots off the floating arena. Last fighter standing wins coins. Punches add knockback percent, so the higher it climbs the further they fly.

  • Computer
    WASD move, mouse aim and camera, click to punch, Space to dash, Shift to sprint. Press Esc to pause.
  • Phone
    Left thumb joystick moves (push fully to sprint). Drag anywhere else to turn the camera. PUNCH auto-aims, DASH dodges.
  • Hazards
    Hammers swing around the hub, the red bumper slides on the east rim, and flashing tiles vanish after a warning. Dashing makes you briefly untouchable.
