Controls. Gas: W / ↑ (hold) · Steer: A D / ← → · Brake: S / ↓ · Shift up: Space · Nitrous: N / Shift (one tank per race, it never refills mid-race, so upgrade your Nitro tank). Touch: shift up + brake on the left, gas + nitro on the right, ◀ ▶ steer in the middle.
Wait for green before holding gas. Shift in the green zone of the rev bar for a perfect shift (boost + cash bonus); shifting early or hitting the limiter costs speed.
Wheelspin: if your engine makes more power than your tyres can hold, the rear tyres smoke and you go nowhere. Shift up to stop it, and upgrade your Tires to keep up with your engine, turbo, exhaust and weight upgrades. If your tyres are in line with your power, there is no wheelspin. Going faster than your handling also makes the car wander, so keep it in its lane!
