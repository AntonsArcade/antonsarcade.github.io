Metro Siege

Share the 4 letter code with a friend, then press Play. Winner earns 500 coins. Your customised mech shows up in the arena.
WASD move · mouse look · click fire · T transform · Space jump (jet: rise) · C descend · 1 to 4 switch gun · E or click at the shop to enter it · B or Esc open this menu.
Walk to the gold MECH SHOP in the middle of the city and click it to buy guns and upgrades.
For a locked mouse, open the file in its own browser tab (previews inside other pages block mouse lock).
